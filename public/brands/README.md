# Brand artwork

The ordering buttons use the official Swiggy and WhatsApp icon-and-wordmark logos, downloaded from their homepages. Zomato uses its wordmark from Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org), distributed under CC0. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners.

Button logo sources:
- `swiggy-logo.svg`: https://media-assets.swiggy.com/portal/testing/seo-home/Group.svg (Swiggy homepage footer).
- `whatsapp-logo.svg`: inline SVG from https://www.whatsapp.com/ (homepage header), with its supplied green set on the SVG so it also works as a standalone image.
- `zomato.svg`: https://cdn.simpleicons.org/zomato

The original icon-only assets remain available from Simple Icons:
- https://cdn.simpleicons.org/swiggy
- https://cdn.simpleicons.org/zomato
- https://cdn.simpleicons.org/whatsapp

Zomato's SVG viewBox is fitted to the horizontal wordmark for legibility at button size. Brand artwork retains its supplied colours.
